# TTC subway reliability

Finding where Toronto's subway loses the most time to delays, what causes them, and which fix would help most.

## The question

Where and when does the TTC subway lose the most time to delays, why do those delays happen, and which fix
would make the biggest difference for riders?

## Who it's for

The people who can act on the answer:

- **TTC operations and service planning**, who decide where maintenance and staff go.
- **The TTC board and Toronto City Council**, who decide which fixes get funded.
- **Riders and transit advocates**, who push for those decisions.

## Why it matters

Toronto's open data portal already has a dashboard that counts delays. A count doesn't say what a delay costs
or what's behind it. This project traces delays to their causes and puts them in rider-hours lost by station,
so one fix can be weighed against another.

## Data

[TTC Subway Delay Data](https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/ttc-subway-delay-data/), published by the Toronto
Transit Commission on Toronto Open Data and updated monthly. The files run from 2014 to today, with a separate
list of delay cause codes.
